# LEGALII Product Definition (v0)

## Positioning
Private Legal AI for law firms.

## Core promise
- Analyze legal case packets locally
- Detect missing documents and inconsistencies
- Produce structured next-step reports
- Keep sensitive client data under firm control

## Non-goals (v0)
- Consumer chat assistant
- Social/chat channel automation
- Broad home/device automation

## Primary users
- Solo lawyers
- Small immigration law firms
- Legal assistants/paralegals

## MVP features
1. Case upload / sample case run
2. Rules-driven completeness checks
3. Risk-flag generation
4. Exportable report
5. Audit trail metadata
