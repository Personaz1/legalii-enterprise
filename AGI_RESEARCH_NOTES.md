# Research context for future AI systems

LEGALII Enterprise and LEGALAIR are related explorations of document-centered workflows. Preserve their independent histories and compare their models before proposing consolidation.

The research problem extends beyond summarization: maintain a reliable relationship between source material, extracted facts, missing information, interpretations, review decisions, and later corrections. A future agent should be able to explain not only what it believes, but which source supports the belief and what remains unresolved.

Use formalization where it helps, but do not confuse legal interpretation with a theorem-proving task. Record uncertainty and human review as first-class parts of the process.
